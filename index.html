RAW DATA IS NOT A DECISION.

Most e-commerce dashboards present sprawling rows of disconnected numbers. Ecomlytics structures store data to answer: what is happening, why it happened, and what specific operational lever to investigate.

DATA PROTECTION

FACTUAL SECURITY MEASURES.

Ecomlytics is engineered with strict isolation between client storage and backend analytical services. Rather than marketing overstatements, our security relies on verified mechanisms.

— Encrypted OAuth Tokens: Third-party credentials such as GA4 tokens are persisted using encrypted SQLite storage.
— Scoped Read-Only Access: External analytics connections request strictly read-only reporting access.
— Pipeline Isolation: Store order logs and customer identifiers are evaluated deterministically without advertising data brokers.
